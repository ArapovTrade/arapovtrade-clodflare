About Arapov.Trade: trading education, books and resources

Arapov.Trade provides free learning materials on trading and financial markets. Igor Arapov's library, practical course, books and videos take readers from basic terms to reading charts and checking trading decisions. The website has Russian, Ukrainian and English versions.

Purpose of the platform

The project's purpose is to make market education accessible through a connected learning sequence. Arapov's approach focuses on price movement, trading volume, the Wyckoff method and risk control. Articles explain the concepts; the course and chart examples show how they are applied.

You can work through the material independently. Short questions and answers help you find an unfamiliar topic, while the detailed articles explain its mechanism and limits. The knowledge map connects library topics so you can continue studying the question you have chosen.

Related material is available in Fundamentals of Trading on Wikibooks and the Wyckoff-method course on Wikiversity. These are additional reading formats, not a substitute for practising with charts.

Learning through the free course

The course starts with a trading platform, a demo account using virtual funds and exchange mechanics. It then introduces charts, trends, price levels and volume, the quantity traded in the selected data source. These lead into entry conditions, trade-size calculations, risk and managing an open trade.

In the practical sections, connect what you observe with a decision: identify the developing price structure, wait for the confirmation required by the rules and calculate trade size from an affordable cash loss. Demo trades help check whether you followed the rules. They do not establish that a real account will receive the same execution or result.

The articles explain individual parts of that process. For a sequence with chart examples, follow the free practical trading course through its table of contents.

Research on investment decisions

Psychology of Investment Decisions: Cognitive Biases of Retail Traders in Financial Markets was published in the journal Інвестиції: практика та досвід. Its authors are I. P. Sytnyk and I. V. Arapov. DOI: 10.32702/2306-6814.2026.4.96.

From Tilt to System: A Practitioner's Framework for Managing Cognitive Biases in Retail Trading is a preprint on SSRN, a working research paper. It proposes a framework informed by the author's practice for managing cognitive biases, recurring errors in judgement, in retail trading. DOI: 10.2139/ssrn.6254718. Further materials are listed in the SSRN author profile.
