How to Use This Section

  1. Choose a topic or find a familiar term in the section description.
  2. On the topic page, open a question and read the short answer.
  3. Follow the link to the detailed article to explore explanations and examples.
  4. If an answer includes a video explanation, use the link to the corresponding lesson chapter.

The topics are connected: understanding orders helps explain liquidity, price analysis is complemented by volume, and a trading system combines entry, exit and risk-control rules. When studying a question, it is useful to explore related library materials.

About the Library Materials

This section is based on materials from the ArapovTrade library and Igor Arapov’s educational videos. Here you can find definitions, comparisons of instruments, explanations of market mechanics, and discussions of the author’s approach to trading.

The library’s author is Igor Arapov, a practicing trader since 2013. The in-depth materials provide context for each question; the short answers serve as a starting point for exploring the topic.
