Getting started and trading systems

Start here to explore trading as a profession: learning, choosing a broker and platform, demo practice, trading styles and developing rules. These resources provide a starting sequence from studying the market to organizing your first trades.

  • Trading for Beginners: Where to Start
  • What to Check Before Opening a Broker Account
  • The Components Every Trading System Needs

Risk and psychology

This area connects capital management with trader behavior: position sizing, stop-losses, trade management, drawdowns, discipline and emotional decisions. Study it alongside a trading system so that entry rules are accompanied by limits on losses and a process for managing decisions.

  • How to Protect Your Capital From Big Losses
  • When to Cut Losses and When to Let Winners Run
  • How Fear and Greed Control Your Trading Decisions

Charts and technical analysis

Learn to read price movement through candles and bars, trends, levels, chart patterns and candlestick patterns. The area also covers breakouts, false breakouts, Elliott waves and Fibonacci levels. Begin with chart structure before moving on to patterns and their context.

  • Reading Price Charts Step by Step
  • How to Read Japanese Candlestick Patterns
  • Recognizing the Most Reliable Chart Patterns

Indicators

Explore tools for measuring trends, momentum and volatility. The library includes MA, ATR, ADX, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, VWAP, Bollinger Bands and Ichimoku. The overview introduces the tools; ATR and moving averages offer two starting perspectives: the size of price fluctuations and the direction of movement.

  • Which Trading Indicators Actually Work
  • How to Use ATR to Measure Volatility
  • Simple vs Exponential Moving Averages Explained

How financial markets work

Understand where and how trading takes place: exchanges, market participants, orders, supply, demand and financial instruments. Further resources in this area cover market microstructure, Forex, fundamental analysis, bonds, and the differences between spot markets and derivatives.

  • What Happens When You Place an Order
  • Market, Limit and Stop Orders: What's the Difference
  • Stocks, Forex, Crypto, Commodities: What's the Difference

Volume, Wyckoff and Smart Money

Connect price, trading volume and the behavior of large market participants. The starting resources move from reading volume to Wyckoff phases and Smart Money concepts. Related material covers liquidity, order blocks and FVG imbalances; the articles provide context for the author’s interpretations.

  • Why Volume Confirms or Denies Price Moves
  • How the Wyckoff Method Reveals Institutional Activity
  • How Institutions Move the Market

Cryptocurrency mechanics and security

Explore blockchain, crypto transactions, wallets and custody. Additional topics include stablecoins, DeFi, staking, tokenomics, arbitrage and crypto derivatives. This starting route combines an understanding of the technology with transaction security and awareness of scams and other risks.

  • How Crypto Actually Works
  • Hot Wallet vs Cold Wallet: Which Do You Need
  • How to Spot a Crypto Scam Before You Lose Money

Bitcoin, altcoins and market cycles

Study individual cryptoassets and their market relationships: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and XRP, halving, ETFs, BTC dominance and ETH/BTC. Begin with Bitcoin and Ethereum, then explore dominance to connect the properties of individual assets with the wider crypto market.

  • What Makes Bitcoin Different From Regular Money
  • What Ethereum Does Beyond Being a Coin
  • What Bitcoin Dominance Tells You About Altcoin Season
