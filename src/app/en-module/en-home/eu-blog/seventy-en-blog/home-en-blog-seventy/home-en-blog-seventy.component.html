1. Understanding the Bitcoin market

Bitcoin basics

What makes Bitcoin different from many other cryptocurrencies?

It has a fixed supply limit of 21 million coins and a defined issuance schedule. That does not prevent large price fluctuations or trading losses.

Bitcoin transactions and mining

How do Bitcoin transactions work?

Keys authorise transfers. Miners group transactions into blocks and use Proof of Work to add them to the chain.

He watches important price levels and waits for a market reaction. A fall that makes the price look cheap is not enough by itself.

No. He uses the halving as background and follows the price trend that develops. The calendar date is not an automatic entry signal.

2. BTC issuance and market cycles

Bitcoin halving

What is a Bitcoin halving?

It halves the subsidy paid for each new block after every 210,000 blocks, roughly four years. This reduces the rate at which new BTC enters circulation.

Read the full explanation

Halving and miners

How does a halving affect miners?

It cuts the new-coin subsidy per block. Miners' results also depend on the Bitcoin price, fees, operating costs and network conditions.

Read the full explanation

Bitcoin market cycles

Does a halving guarantee a new bull market?

No. The library examines historical cycles, but events occurring together do not prove that the same price pattern must repeat.

Read the full explanation

2024 Bitcoin halving

What changed at the 2024 Bitcoin halving?

The block subsidy fell from 6.25 BTC to 3.125 BTC. This is a historical supply change, not a guarantee of a particular price outcome.

Read the full explanation

Halving trading myths

Why can trading only on the halving story fail?

The date is known well in advance, so expectations may already affect prices. Arapov looks at levels and volume rather than relying on the story alone.

Read the full explanation

3. Bitcoin exposure through funds

Bitcoin ETF

What do I own when I buy a Bitcoin ETF?

You own a share in a fund through a brokerage account. You do not directly hold the fund's Bitcoin keys or use that holding for an on-chain payment.

Read the full explanation

Types of Bitcoin funds

How do spot, futures and income-oriented Bitcoin funds differ?

Spot funds hold Bitcoin; futures funds use contracts. Income-oriented strategies may exchange some upside for payments. The fund's structure matters more than its label.

Read the full explanation

US spot ETF launch

When did US spot Bitcoin ETFs receive approval?

The library discusses the January 2024 approval of US spot products. It uses that event to explain an additional route for institutional and brokerage-account exposure.

Read the full explanation

ETF vs direct Bitcoin

How is a Bitcoin ETF different from holding BTC yourself?

An ETF offers price exposure through fund shares. Direct ownership gives control of the keys and access to on-chain transfers, with different storage responsibilities.

Read the full explanation

Bitcoin ETF flows

What do Bitcoin ETF inflows and outflows show?

They help describe demand for fund exposure and associated asset movements. Arapov uses flows as background rather than a precise entry signal.

Read the full explanation

ETF creations and redemptions

How are ETF shares linked to the underlying Bitcoin?

Authorised participants create or redeem shares through the fund's process. This connects share supply with underlying assets and helps prices stay near net asset value.

4. Understanding the Ethereum market

Ethereum vs Bitcoin

How is Ethereum different from Bitcoin?

Ethereum supports applications and smart contracts and uses Proof of Stake. Its supply design differs from Bitcoin's fixed cap.

Read the full explanation

ETH, gas and staking

What is ETH used for?

ETH pays for network operations, called gas, and supports validation through staking. Ethereum's move to Proof of Stake and its fee-burning mechanism are separate parts of its design.

Read the full explanation

Smart contracts

What is a smart contract?

It is code that carries out rules on a blockchain. Bugs or unsafe design can put funds at risk, so automatic execution does not mean the code is safe.

Read the full explanation

Ethereum chart analysis

How does Arapov analyse ETH?

He studies levels, volume and false breaks as with other markets. He also considers the limitations of volume data from a particular trading venue.

Read the full explanation

ETH/BTC ratio

What does the ETH/BTC ratio show?

It shows Ethereum's price relative to Bitcoin. Arapov uses it to assess relative strength, alongside BTC's own movement, rather than as a standalone entry signal.

Read the full explanation

Ethereum trading risk

What risks does Arapov consider when trading ETH?

He considers volatility, liquidity and position size. A strong story about the ecosystem does not remove the risk of a losing trade.

Read the full explanation

Ethereum Layer 2

Why does Ethereum use Layer 2 networks?

They handle part of transaction processing outside the base layer. Rollups group transactions and submit data or results to Ethereum, helping reduce congestion and costs.

Solana vs Ethereum

How does Solana differ from Ethereum?

The library compares Solana's focus on speed and low costs with Ethereum's approach and ecosystem. For a trader, SOL still needs to be assessed as a volatile asset.

6. XRP, payments and the ledger

XRP payments

What is XRP used for?

XRP is the native asset of XRP Ledger and can be used in transfers and as a bridge asset. Its initial supply was created rather than issued through mining.

Read the full explanation

Ripple vs XRP

How are Ripple, XRP and XRP Ledger different?

Ripple is a company, XRP is an asset and XRP Ledger is the blockchain. They are related names, but they are not interchangeable.

Read the full explanation

XRP Ledger validation

How does XRP Ledger work without mining?

Validators agree on transactions through its consensus process rather than Proof of Work mining. That supports the ledger's payment-focused design.

Read the full explanation

XRP price levels

How does Arapov look at XRP levels?

He checks price structure and visible psychological levels, then examines the reaction. He does not treat a round price or volume spike alone as an entry.

XRP regulatory risk

Why does regulatory history matter for XRP?

The library discusses how regulatory disputes affected attention and trading. Arapov also considers volatility and concentrated holdings rather than assuming one legal event removes all risk.

7. Bitcoin and the wider crypto market

Bitcoin dominance

What does Bitcoin dominance measure?

It is Bitcoin's share of the cryptocurrency market value measured by a data provider. It describes relative market weight rather than the direction of BTC's price.

Read the full explanation

Dominance vs price

Can Bitcoin dominance rise while BTC falls?

Yes. Dominance is a relative share, so it can rise if other assets fall more. It is different from whether Bitcoin's own price rises or falls.

Read the full explanation

Altseason

How does Arapov use dominance to assess altcoins?

He watches whether BTC's market share is rising or falling as context for relative altcoin strength. Individual entries still need price and volume assessment.

Read the full explanation

Dominance data limits

Can Bitcoin dominance change without a Bitcoin trade?

Yes. Changes in other token values, supply or the data provider's coverage can change the percentage. It is not a direct measure of money flowing into Bitcoin.

Read the full explanation

Dominance as a filter

Can dominance tell me exactly when to buy?

No. Arapov uses it as a market filter. He takes timing from price reactions and volume rather than each small change in the percentage.

Read the full explanation
